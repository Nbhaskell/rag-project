# Bluefin Coffee Roasters: Employee Handbook

Welcome to Bluefin Coffee Roasters, a small roastery founded in 2014 in Astoria, Oregon by Marisol Quenby and Tobias Larkin. We employ 23 people across our roastery, our two cafes, and our online shop.

## Working Hours

The roastery floor opens at 5:30 AM so the first roast batch can start by 6:00 AM. Cafe staff work in two shifts: the morning shift runs 6:30 AM to 2:00 PM and the afternoon shift runs 1:30 PM to 9:00 PM. Office staff work 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM with a flexible start time of up to one hour either way.

All employees receive a 30-minute unpaid lunch break and two paid 10-minute breaks per shift.

## Paid Time Off

New employees receive 12 days of paid time off in their first year. After two full years, this increases to 18 days. After five years, it increases to 24 days. PTO requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the Bluefin Hub scheduling app, except for sick days.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Every employee receives 6 paid sick days per year. Unused sick days do not roll over, but unused PTO can carry over up to a maximum of 5 days.

## The Free Coffee Policy

Every employee may take home one free 12-ounce bag of coffee per week. Staff can also drink unlimited coffee during shifts. Staff who want to try a new roast before it goes on sale can join the "Cupping Club," which meets every Thursday at 3:00 PM in the roastery tasting room.

## Roasting Schedule

We roast on a fixed weekly schedule so our beans are always fresh when they ship:
- Monday: Ethiopian Yirgacheffe and Colombian Huila
- Tuesday: Our house blend, called "Harbor Light"
- Wednesday: Decaf (Swiss Water process) and Guatemalan Antigua
- Thursday: Our dark roast, called "Midnight Tide"
- Friday: Small-batch experimental roasts only

Online orders placed before 2:00 PM Pacific ship the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ship the next business day. We never ship coffee that was roasted more than 5 days earlier.

## Equipment and Safety

Our main roaster is a 15-kilogram Probat drum roaster named "Big Gretchen." Only employees who have completed the 20-hour roaster certification may operate it. The smaller 5-kilogram sample roaster is nicknamed "Pip" and may be used by any trained employee.

All employees must wear closed-toe shoes on the roastery floor. Hearing protection is required during grinding and packaging. The fire extinguisher is mounted next to the back door, and the emergency meeting point is the parking lot behind the building, beside the blue dumpster.

## Expense Reimbursement

Employees can be reimbursed for work-related expenses of up to $75 without prior approval. Anything over $75 needs written approval from your manager first. Submit receipts within 30 days through the Bluefin Hub app. Reimbursements are paid on the 15th of the following month.
