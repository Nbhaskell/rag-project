# Bluefin Coffee Roasters Employee Handbook

Bluefin Coffee Roasters is a small made-up company used for testing. This handbook is fictional.

## Working hours
The roastery is open Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Full-time staff work 8-hour shifts with a 30-minute unpaid lunch break. The roastery is closed on Sundays.

## Time off
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation requests must be submitted to the shift manager at least two weeks in advance. Sick days are separate and do not count against vacation. Each employee gets 6 paid sick days per year.

## Roasting schedule
Light roasts are done on Mondays and Wednesdays. Dark roasts are done on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Fridays are reserved for special small-batch orders. Saturday mornings are for packaging and shipping.

## Equipment safety
All roasting staff must wear heat-resistant gloves and closed-toe shoes. The chaff collector must be emptied after every roast. Fire extinguishers are located next to the main roaster and by the back door. Report any equipment problems to Marta Delgado, the head roaster, immediately.

## Expenses
Employees may be reimbursed for work-related purchases under $50 without prior approval. Anything over $50 needs written approval from the owner, Priya Raman. Submit receipts within 14 days.
