# Tidepool Trails Visitor Guide

Tidepool Trails is a made-up coastal nature park used for testing. This guide is fictional.

## Opening hours and fees
The park is open daily from sunrise to sunset. Entry costs $8 per adult and $4 per child aged 5 to 12. Children under 5 enter free. An annual pass costs $60 and covers one household.

## Trails
- **Gull Point Loop**: 2 miles, easy. Takes about 1 hour. Best for families. Dogs on leash are welcome.
- **Kelp Ridge Trail**: 4.5 miles, moderate. Takes about 2.5 hours. Steep section near the halfway point. No dogs allowed.
- **Anemone Cove Path**: 1 mile, easy. Only open at low tide. Check the tide chart at the visitor center before heading out.

## Wildlife
Harbor seals rest on the rocks near Gull Point from May to September. Please stay at least 50 yards away. Sea stars and anemones can be seen at Anemone Cove, but visitors must not touch or remove them.

## Rules
Camping is not allowed anywhere in the park. Fires and barbecues are prohibited. Drones may not be flown without a permit from the park office. Please pack out all trash.

## Contact
The visitor center is open 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Park ranger Tomas Okafor can be reached at the front desk.
