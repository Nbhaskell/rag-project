# Barks n Woofs: Vaccination and Health Requirements

This document explains which vaccinations and health conditions are required for a dog to attend Barks n Woofs.

## Required Vaccinations

Every dog must have current proof of the following vaccinations before their first visit:
- Rabies (valid for 3 years, depending on the vaccine given)
- DHPP, also called distemper/parvo (valid for 1 year after the booster series)
- Bordetella, also called kennel cough (must be given within the last 12 months)
- Canine influenza: recommended

## How to Provide Proof

Owners must send vaccination records from their veterinarian by email address,  in person or by online form. Records must show the dog's name, the vaccine, the date given, and the date it expires. Photos of paper records are not accepted.

## Expired Vaccinations

If a dog's vaccination expires, the dog cannot attend until updated proof is received. We do allow a grace period of 5 days.

## Flea and Tick Policy

Dogs must be on a flea and tick preventive. A dog found with fleas or ticks will be sent home and the owner will be notified.

## Sick Dog Policy

Do not bring your dog if they have had any of these in the last 48 hours: vomiting, diarrhea, coughing, sneezing, eye or nose discharge, fever, or open wounds. Dogs showing signs of illness during the day will be separated and the owner will be called for pickup within 1 hour. A dog who has been ill may return after 2 days, or with a vet's note.

## Spay and Neuter Requirements

Dogs over 6 months old must be spayed or neutered to attend. Female dogs in heat cannot attend.

## Puppies

Puppies must be at least 4 months old and have completed their puppy vaccination series before attending.

## Medications

Staff can give medications. If we can, owners must provide the medication in its original container with written instructions, and we charge $10 per dose.
