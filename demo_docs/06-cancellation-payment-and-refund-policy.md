# Barks n Woofs: Cancellation, Payment and Refund Policy

## Cancelling a Daycare Day

Cancel at least 12 hours before drop-off by phone, email or text to avoid a charge.
- Cancelling on time: no charge, and a package day is returned.
- Late cancellation or no-show: charged a fee of $50.00, and a package day is used.

## Cancelling Boarding

- Cancel 5 days or more before arrival: full refund of any deposit.
- Cancel less than 5 days before arrival: deposit is partly refunded.
- Holiday boarding requires cancellation at least 10 days ahead. Late cancellations are charged $10.00.

## Deposits

Boarding reservations require a deposit of $25.00, due at booking. The deposit is applied to the final bill.

## Packages and Memberships

- Package days are non-refundable after purchase, except if the dog is permanently removed by us.
- Packages expire 90 days after purchase unless stated otherwise.
- Monthly memberships renew automatically on 1st of each month. Cancel at least 5 days before renewal to avoid the next charge.

## Payment

- Payment methods accepted: Cash, Check, Card.
- Payment is due at drop-off or when billed.
- Late or failed payments are charged a fee of $10.00. Dogs may not attend until the balance is paid.

## Refunds

- Refund requests must be made within 5 days of the charge.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- If we close because of weather or an emergency, we give a refund.
- If a dog is sent home early for illness or an incident, we refund the unused portion.

## Disputes

Questions about a charge should go to Nathan at 111-111-1111 or email@address.com within 5 days.
