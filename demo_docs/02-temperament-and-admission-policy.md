# Barks n Woofs: Temperament Evaluation and Admission Policy

This document explains how a new dog is accepted at Barks n Woofs, and when a dog may be declined or removed.

## Who Can Attend

We welcome dogs who are 4 months and older, up to 250 pounds, who are healthy, vaccinated, and comfortable around other dogs and people. Separate play groups are organized by size, temperament and age.

## The Temperament Evaluation

Every new dog must complete a temperament evaluation before their first full day.
- **Cost:** free of cost.
- **Length:** about 1 hour.
- **What happens:** a staff member meets the owner and dog, then the dog is introduced to a calm, friendly dog, and then to a small group. Staff watch for comfort, play style, and reactions to handling, noise, and other dogs.
- **Results:** the dog is either approved for group play, approved with conditions (for example, small groups only), or not approved. We explain the result to the owner after the evaluation.

Owners should bring: vaccination records, the signed owner agreement, and the dog's vet contact information.

## Reasons a Dog May Be Declined or Removed

- Aggression toward people or dogs, including biting or attempted biting
- Resource guarding that cannot be managed (food, toys, or people)
- Extreme fear or anxiety that does not improve after 10 visits
- Excessive barking, destructiveness, or an inability to settle despite staff help
- Illness or lack of current vaccinations (see the Health Requirements document)
- Repeated incidents, as described below

## Incident and Removal Policy

- After a 1st incident, the owner is notified and the dog may be separated for the rest of the day.
- After 3 incidents within 5 days, the dog is suspended for 1 day then re-evaluated or removed.
- Staff have the final decision. We put the safety of every dog and team member first.

## Re-evaluation

A dog who was declined may be re-evaluated after 30 days or 1 month.
