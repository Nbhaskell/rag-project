# Barks n Woofs: Hours and Schedule

## Regular Hours

- Monday to Friday: 8:00am to 6:00pm
- Saturday: 8:00am to 6:00pm
- Sunday: 8:00am to 6:00pm

## Drop-Off and Pick-Up Windows

- Morning drop-off: between 8:00am and 9:00am
- Midday pick-up: 11:00am and 1:00pm (half-day dogs)
- Evening pick-up: between 5:30pm and 6:00pm

Dogs dropped off after 9:00am may be charged for a full day.
Pickup after closing time incurs a late fee. See the Pricing document.

## Holidays

We are closed on: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day.
We have modified hours on: No Days.
Holiday boarding and daycare may require reservations 30 days prior to boarding.

## Bad Weather and Emergencies

If we close or delay opening because of snow, ice, storms, power outages, we will notify owners by text, email, social media or phone as soon as possible.
Owners with confirmed reservations are refunded or given a credit for days we close.

## Reservations

- Daycare reservations: required
- Boarding reservations: required, at least 3 days ahead
- Reserve by phone, website or email
