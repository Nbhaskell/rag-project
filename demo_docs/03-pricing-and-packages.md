# Barks n Woofs: Pricing and Packages

All prices are in USD and are effective 01/01/2026. Prices may change with 30 days notice.

## Daycare Rates

- Full day (over 4 hours): $100.00
- Half day (up to 4 hours): $50.00
- Second dog from the same household: $50 per day

## Packages

- 5-day package: $450, valid for 90 days
- 10-day package: $800, valid for 90 days
- 20-day package: $1,500, valid for 90 days
- Unlimited monthly: $2,500 per month with terms

Unused days can be transferred to another dog.

## Boarding (overnight stays) 

- Standard overnight: $125.00 per night
- Second dog sharing a room: $50.00 per night
- Holiday surcharge: $25.00 per night on listed holidays.
- Check-in is between 8:00am and 9:00am. Check-out is by 6:00pm.

## Add-Ons

- Bath: $50.00
- Nail trim: $30.00
- Extra meal or medication administration: $10.00
- Training session: $100.00

## Fees

- Temperament evaluation: Free
- Late pickup: $5.00 for every 30 minutes after closing time
- Returned payment fee: $10.00
- Cleanup fee for accidents or illness: $10.00

## Discounts

- Multi-dog, military, rescue, or referral discounts: 10%.

## Payment

Payment is due at drop-off or when billed. We accept cash, check, cards.
