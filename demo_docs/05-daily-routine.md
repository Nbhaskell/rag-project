# Barks n Woofs: A Typical Day

This document describes what a dog's day looks like at Barks n Woofs. Times are approximate and may change with weather and the dogs in each group.

## Schedule

- 8:00 AM to 9:00 AM Arrival. Dogs are greeted, checked for health, and put in a play group.
- 9:00 AM to 11:30 AM Morning play: supervised group play in the outdoor yard.
- 11:30 AM to 12:00 PM Lunch and water break. Dogs who need a meal are fed in separate spaces.
- 12:00 PM to 2:30 PM Nap time. Dogs rest in individual or shared quiet areas.
- 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM Afternoon play, enrichment, and outdoor time.
- 4:30 PM to 6:00 PM Pick-up time. Dogs are brought to the lobby with a short report.

## Play Groups

Dogs are grouped by size, age, energy level, and play style. A staff member supervises each group at a ratio of 1 staff member for every 5 dogs.

## Feeding

- Bring meals in a labeled container with the dog's name, portion, and any instructions.
- We do not feed outside food unless it is provided by the owner.
- Water is always available.
- Treats are only given with owner permission.

## Rest and Enrichment

Rest periods are scheduled so that dogs do not get overtired. Enrichment includes puzzle toys, sniff games, water play, and training practice.

## Report Cards and Photos

Owners receive a daily report with notes on meals, bathroom breaks, and behavior by email. Photos are shared daily.

## What to Bring

- Food and medications, labeled
- A collar with ID tag
- A leash, toys, or a blanket: allowed
